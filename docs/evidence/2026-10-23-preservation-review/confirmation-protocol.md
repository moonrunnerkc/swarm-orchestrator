# Confirmation after the fresh block, registered before its first launch

October 23, 2026. Not a fresh result. The fresh block's three tasks are exposed: their hidden
verdicts were revealed and the implementer read their ledgers to find the defect this checks. The
block's own counts stand as published and are not replaced by anything here.

## What it checks

The fresh block found every Swarm candidate the hidden evaluator passes refused by the product
with its repair attempts unused. The reason: a reading the change itself left unmeasured was
labelled `setup unavailable`, and the repair policy stops on setup. The correction (amendment 10)
labels it `unmeasured by the change`. This confirmation asks two questions only:

1. Does repair now proceed in those runs? Measured from the ledger: attempts used, and the stop
   reason.
2. What do the product's verdict and the hidden evaluator say about what it delivers?

## Launches

- Tasks: `ufo-feature-filter-query`, `ufo-bugfix-base-prefix-boundary`,
  `nanoid-refactor-module-subdirectories`.
- Arms: `swarm` (`--adaptive --review executable`) and `swarm-control` (`--adaptive`).
- Samples: one each, six launches.
- Conditions: the block's budgets, model and order rule, on the build that carries the correction.
- Every launch runs, and nothing changes between launches.

## Reading

- Per launch:
  - attempts used and the stop reason;
  - the product's verdict and the hidden verdict;
  - complete delivery by the registered definition;
  - whether the review examined.
- A repair that changes a file the task did not ask for, such as reformatting `src/punycode.ts`
  to clear the base's own lint failure, is reported as such.
- With one sample per cell, a difference of one or two is within chance.
